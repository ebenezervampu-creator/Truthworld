## 3. Statutory Takedown Notice Requirements (17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3))

To be effective under global copyright laws, a notification must be in writing and include:
1. **Signature:** A physical or electronic signature of a person authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.
2. **Identification of Work:** Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed, or a representative list of works.
3. **Identification of Infringing Material:** Identification of the material that is claimed to be infringing (including the exact TruthWorld URL or Video ID).
4. **Contact Information:** Full name, address, telephone number, and email address.
5. **Good Faith Statement:** A statement that the complaining party has a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
6. **Accuracy Statement:** A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that the complaining party is authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.

## 4. Counter-Notification Procedure (17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(3))

If a creator believes that their video was disabled or removed by mistake or misidentification (e.g., fair use, public domain, or valid license), they may submit a Counter-Notification containing:
1. Physical or electronic signature.
2. Identification of the material removed and its location before removal.
3. A statement under penalty of perjury that the creator has a good faith belief the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification.
4. Name, address, and telephone number, and consent to the jurisdiction of the Federal District Court (for US residents) or competent courts in India/Telangana.

Upon receipt of a valid counter-notification, TruthWorld forwards it to the original claimant. If the claimant does not initiate court action within 10 to 14 business days, access to the video may be restored.
