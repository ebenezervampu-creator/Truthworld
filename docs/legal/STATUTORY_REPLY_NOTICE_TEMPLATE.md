### I. LEGAL STATUS: INTERMEDIARY SAFE HARBOR UNDER SECTION 79 OF THE IT ACT, 2000

1. **Intermediary Status:** TruthWorld is an electronic video-sharing platform operating strictly as an **"Intermediary"** as defined under **Section 2(1)(w)** of the Information Technology Act, 2000.
2. **Statutory Immunity (Safe Harbor):** Under **Section 79(1)** of the Information Technology Act, 2000:
   > *"An intermediary shall not be liable for any third party information, data, or communication link made available or hosted by him."*
3. **No Modification of User Content:** In compliance with Section 79(2)(b), TruthWorld did not initiate the transmission, did not select the recipient of the transmission, and has not modified the source audiovisual file uploaded by the user.
4. **Binding Precedent of the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India:** As laid down by the Hon’ble Supreme Court of India in the landmark case of ***Shreya Singhal v. Union of India*** *(2015) 5 SCC 1*, an intermediary is protected by law and cannot be subjected to criminal liability for user-generated content in the absence of **"actual knowledge"** conveyed through an order of a Court of competent jurisdiction or a lawful government directive issued under Section 69A of the IT Act.
