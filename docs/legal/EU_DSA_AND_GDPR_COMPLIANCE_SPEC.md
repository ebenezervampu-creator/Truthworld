# TruthWorld EU Digital Services Act (DSA) & GDPR Compliance Specification
**(Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 & Regulation (EU) 2016/679)**

---

## 1. Scope & Application

TruthWorld provides video-sharing and creator hosting services to users globally, including across the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom. This document outlines TruthWorld’s compliance with the **EU Digital Services Act (DSA)** and **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**.

---

## 2. DSA Statutory Points of Contact

In compliance with **Articles 11 and 12 of the DSA**:
* **Single Point of Contact for Authorities:**
  - Authorities of Member States, the European Commission, and the European Board for Digital Services may contact:  
    `hello@truthworld.in` (Subject: `[DSA Authority Request]`)
  - Languages of Communication: English.
* **Single Point of Contact for Recipients of the Service:**
  - Platform users may submit inquiries and reports directly via:  
    `hello@truthworld.in` or in-app support channels.

---

## 3. Moderation & Fact-Checking Redressal Under the DSA

### A. Internal Complaint-Handling System (Article 20 DSA)
1. TruthWorld provides an internal complaint-handling system for a period of at least **six (6) months** following any decision to:
   - Remove or disable access to content.
   - Suspend or terminate accounts or monetization.
   - Display a corrective fact-check overlay or dispute status.
2. Appeals are managed via the in-app Creator Studio or by emailing `hello@truthworld.in`.
3. Decisions are made objectively and non-arbitrarily without sole reliance on automated processing.

### B. Out-of-Court Dispute Settlement (Article 21 DSA)
1. Recipients of the service in the EU who are dissatisfied with an internal complaint decision are entitled to select any **Certified Out-of-Court Dispute Settlement Body** established under Article 21(3) of the DSA.
2. TruthWorld engages with certified dispute settlement bodies in good faith to resolve content moderation or fact-checking rating disagreements.

### C. Trusted Flaggers (Article 22 DSA)
- TruthWorld provides prioritized processing for notices submitted by recognized, certified **Trusted Flaggers** across the European Union.

---

## 4. Cross-Border GDPR Data Transfers & Standard Contractual Clauses

1. **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs):** Transfers of personal data from the EEA to cloud storage and database facilities outside the EEA are governed by European Commission-approved Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2: Controller-to-Processor and Module 1: Controller-to-Controller).
2. **Data Subject Rights:** Full compliance with GDPR Articles 15 through 22 (Access, Rectification, Erasure, Data Portability, and Objection to Automated Profiling).
3. **Data Protection Officer / Representative:** Inquiries can be submitted to `hello@truthworld.in`.
